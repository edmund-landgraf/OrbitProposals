THE ECONOMIC DECISION

Free gets attention.
Governance earns expansion.

We should not try to monetize the first moment of curiosity. Let people create a real proposal and experience the difference.

Charge when they need more authors, more workspaces, approval controls, integrations, API access, research volume, or downstream handoff.

That keeps Orbit accessible against PandaDoc’s free plan and low-cost proposal tools while reserving expansion revenue for the parts that create operational value.
