THE PRODUCT

One system for the work between “sounds good” and “let’s sign.”

Orbit Proposal is the operating layer for teams that sell expertise: structured intake, reusable scope, governed pricing, review lanes, and a clean handoff to delivery.

WHAT IT REPLACES

The invisible work
between the lines.

Most proposal processes are held together by memory. A form collects one piece of context, a spreadsheet stores pricing, a document holds the language, and an email thread decides who can approve the exception.

Orbit turns those fragments into a workspace your team can run repeatedly. Every proposal has an owner, a shape, a decision trail, and a next step.
