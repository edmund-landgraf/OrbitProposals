HOW IT WORKS

Stop asking prospects to describe a future they cannot yet see.

Give them a place to think out loud, bring in the right context, and arrive with something your team can evaluate — not just another form submission.

THE SHIFT

From a contact form
to a working brief.

The old motion collects intent and creates another round of translation. The new motion helps someone compose a meaningful starting point before the first human conversation.

THE OLD MOTION

“Tell us about your project.”

Visitor fills in boxes→Salesperson qualifies→Call gets booked

The team receives a thin description, then spends the first call recovering context that could have been captured before anyone met.

THE NEW MOTION

“Compose a proposal with us.”

Client describes the need→Agent researches + shapes→Architect reviews the work

The first conversation starts further downstream: with assumptions, references, risks, and a possible shape for the work already on the table.

01 / COMPOSE

A better starting point is already useful.

Someone arrives with a goal, a system they already own, or a problem they can describe in plain language. They do not need to know your service catalog or the right technical vocabulary.

They can mention the strange legacy application, the awkward data export, the half-working workflow, or the business constraint that matters most. The system helps turn that raw material into a structured brief.

02 / ARRIVE

Bring the architect a composed problem.

By the time a human joins, the conversation is no longer “what do you do?” It is “is this the right path, what did we miss, and what should we do first?”

That changes the role of the expert. Less qualification theater. More judgment, risk review, and useful decisions.

03 / DECIDE

The seller gets to evaluate the work, too.

A proposal is not just a buying interface. It is a fit filter for both sides.

  • See whether the request fits your capabilities.
  • Understand the unusual systems before committing.
  • Price the real shape of the work, not a guess.
  • Decline mismatched work with clarity and respect.
