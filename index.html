Not sure where Orbit fits? Start with the questions we hear most.

Is this another AI writing tool? ＋

No. Orbit is the operating layer around the writing: intake, scoping, pricing, reviews, approvals, and handoff. AI helps draft the work while your rules stay in charge.

Can we use our own proposal templates? ＋

Yes. Bring your service catalog, pricing rules, terms, brand voice, and review steps. Orbit is designed to fit how your team already sells.

Who is it for? ＋

Boutique agencies, consultancies, studios, and internal services teams who want a faster path from a messy request to a confident, deliverable proposal.

How does onboarding work? ＋

Start with one workflow. We help map your intake, pricing, and approval boundaries, then your team can run the next proposal through the workspace.
