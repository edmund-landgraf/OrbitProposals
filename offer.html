WHAT WE OFFER

A proposal operating system for the work before the contract.

Orbit helps a buyer compose a better request and helps a provider turn it into a bounded, reviewable commitment.

THE OFFER, IN PLAIN TERMS

Not another document editor.
A decision system.

Most proposal tools start with a document. Orbit starts earlier and carries the decision further.

PRODUCT LAYERS

One front door.
Six useful layers.

01

Proposal intake

A conversational starting point for the buyer. Plain language is enough; the system helps make it specific.

02

Knowledge + research

Reference your catalog, prior work, source documents, and the long tail of software your team may encounter.

03

Scope composer

Build the offer from deliverables, milestones, assumptions, options, and exclusions instead of freeform prose alone.

04

Commercial rules

Make rate cards, margins, caps, discounts, payment terms, and exceptions explicit and enforceable.

05

Review + approval

Route the right decision to the right person and keep comments, flags, and decisions with the proposal.

06

Commitment handoff

Move the approved commitment into contract, billing, project, milestone, and task records.

THE ADVANTAGE

More signal before the call.
More judgment during it.

Orbit does not try to eliminate the expert. It gives the expert a better starting point.

The buyer can arrive with a composed problem. The provider can decide whether the work is a fit, what risk needs verification, and what the smallest useful first step should be.

That is the offer: less qualification theater, more informed decisions, and a better handoff when both sides say yes.
