WHERE ORBIT LANDS

The proposal operating system for complex services.

Orbit is for work that cannot be reduced to a product catalog, a blank contact form, or a polished PDF.

THE POSITIONING

For agencies, consultancies, implementation partners, and internal services teams, Orbit is the AI-native proposal operating system that turns an ambiguous request into a governed commitment. Unlike document-first proposal tools, Orbit starts with the buyer’s real problem and carries the approved decision into delivery.
