THE COMMON THREAD

The buyer can do more work before the call.

That does not mean every buyer has to become a technical architect. It means the system can meet them where they are, fill in context they do not have, and give them something concrete to react to.

By the time a human joins, both sides have more signal. The provider can decide whether the work is a fit. The buyer can decide whether the provider understands the problem.
