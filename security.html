TRUST + CONTROL

Fast drafts. Deliberate decisions.

Orbit is designed so assistance can move quickly without becoming the source of truth.

01

Permissioned workspaces

Keep teams, clients, and internal review lanes separated with explicit workspace and role boundaries.

02

Server-side rules

Pricing, access, and approval conditions live in application logic, not in a prompt or a shared document.

03

Traceable actions

Keep a record of who changed what, when a proposal was reviewed, and which version was approved.

04

Data minimization

Bring the context needed for the decision. Keep unrelated customer or financial data out of the workflow.

05

Human checkpoints

Make exceptions visible and require the right person to approve them before commitment.

06

Portable records

Your proposal data should remain understandable beyond one interface or one model provider.

BOUNDARY MAP

AI assists.
Your system decides.

Orbit treats a model as an assistant at the edge of a controlled application. The assistant can ask questions, suggest language, and assemble a draft. It does not get to silently change a margin, approve an exception, or redefine what was sold.

That boundary is the product.
