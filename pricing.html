to discover the product.

The free plan should be useful enough to build a real proposal. Paid plans should unlock collaboration, governance, volume, integrations, and handoff—not basic understanding.

We should avoid charging for every draft or forcing a team into a high minimum before it has earned confidence in the workflow.
