CATEGORY CONTEXT

Most proposal software optimizes the document. Orbit optimizes the decision.

Commercial tools have made proposals faster to create, easier to brand, simpler to sign, and more measurable. Orbit begins with the next problem: how to make the proposal itself a useful, governed starting point.

WHERE ORBIT WINS

The work before the document is where the leverage lives.

Orbit starts with a better buyer input. The client does not need to know your service taxonomy or the right technical terms.

Orbit turns context into structure. The system can recognize the long tail of software, constraints, dependencies, and risks that a blank form cannot.

Orbit protects the provider. It makes fit, margin, approval, and delivery risk visible before the team commits.

Orbit preserves the commitment. The approved proposal is not the end of the process. It becomes the source for contract, billing, and delivery.
