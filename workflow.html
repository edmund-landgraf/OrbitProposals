THE WORKFLOW

From first signal to signed scope, without the handoff tax.

A proposal is not a document. It is a sequence of decisions. Orbit makes the sequence visible, repeatable, and easier to improve.

01

CAPTURE

Start with the request, not the template.

Collect the context that changes the work: desired outcome, constraints, stakeholders, urgency, and what “done” means.

  • Conversation and form intake
  • Unknowns and follow-up questions
  • Owner and next action
02

SHAPE

Turn expertise into an offer.

Pull from your services, delivery patterns, language, and pricing rules. The draft becomes explainable because its parts are known.

  • Deliverables and exclusions
  • Milestones and assumptions
  • Rate cards, caps, and margins
03

DECIDE

Make review a lane, not a scramble.

Route exceptions and commitments to the people who should see them. Keep notes with the proposal, not in a side channel.

  • Role-based review
  • Approval history
  • Client-ready versioning
04

HAND OFF

Deliver what was actually sold.

When the proposal is approved, its structured scope becomes the starting point for delivery, billing, and customer communication.

  • Scope lock
  • Project kickoff packet
  • Change trail from commitment to delivery

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Let AI move quickly.
Let rules move last.

Orbit uses automation where speed helps and explicit human checkpoints where a proposal becomes a promise.
